The reason we built it

Safety is easier to practise when we have the right words.

Children do not need fear-based lessons. They need clear language, patient adults and the confidence to recognise when something does not feel right — then know what to do next.

Our safeguarding promise

Care is not a footnote.
It is the method.

Hifazat Guide keeps children at the centre while making adult responsibility clear. The content is emotionally safe, the language is culturally respectful, and the route to support never depends on a child sharing more than they choose.

01

Protect privacy
Surveys are anonymous; personal or sensitive disclosures are not collected.

02

Keep trusted adults present
A counsellor or trained teacher supports every session and follows established referral procedures.

03

Escalate responsibly
Concerns are handled through the school’s safeguarding pathway, not by student volunteers.

Learn, listen, improve

Measure what changes.
Protect what is shared.

We look for shifts in awareness, understanding and confidence — never for personal stories. The goal is an evidence-based guide that can be refined without losing its care.
